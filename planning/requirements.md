# The Corner Cafe - Website Requirements

## Top 3 Priority Requirements

- [ ] Show an aligned price for every item in the breakfast, lunch and drinks sections.
- [ ] Show opening hours on all four pages and make them visible on the mobile home page before scrolling.
- [ ] Provide the cafe's address, contact details and a map on the Find Us page.

## Other Requirements

- [ ] Link Home, Menu, Weekly Specials and Find Us from every page. Make the site work on phones, tablets and laptops without sideways scrolling.
- [ ] Create a welcoming home page with a short introduction, a cafe photo area and warm colours.
- [ ] Show the current weekly special with its name and price, and make it easy to update each week.
- [ ] Use clear text contrast and provide suitable alternative text for images.
